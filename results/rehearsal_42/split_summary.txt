Method

  matched pairs plus all pairs scoring above the band (score > effective_threshold + 0.05).
  Components ordered by smallest record id, shuffled with seed 0, assigned to train
  until train holds 55% of records. A pair is kept only if both records are on the
  same side; crossing pairs are excluded from training and scoring.
